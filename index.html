HOW THIS WAS BUILT

Source: raw SwingVision match exports. One row per ball struck, plus the point log. Shots are mirrored into a single court frame in Bruin Tennis Consulting court units, so the BTC court visuals apply unchanged. Point outcomes come from the last ball of each rally. Nothing is tagged by hand.

Season figures pool 11 tracked matches for Johnson and 19 for Quan. The six roster matches are the same kind of shot-level export, one representative match per player, so the net figures are single-match counts — shown in full (won of played) rather than as rates, because the samples are small on purpose: these players rarely come forward. Counts are shown instead of averages wherever the sample is small enough to read directly. Chart colours were checked for colour-blind separation before use.
