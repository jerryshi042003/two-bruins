HOW THIS WAS BUILT

Raw SwingVision match exports — one row per ball (stroke, spin, speed, direction, result, coordinates, video timestamp) plus the point log (score state, break points, point endings, durations). Shots are mirrored into one court frame and converted to Bruin Tennis Consulting court units so the BTC D3 court visuals apply unchanged. Match flow, pressure splits, point-duration economics and the win-rate-by-zone badges are computed from the export — none of them appear in the SwingVision app itself. No manual tagging. Small samples carry their counts.
