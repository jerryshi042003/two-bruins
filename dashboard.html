HOW THIS WAS BUILT

Source: the team's match-tagging database, one tagged point per row. Each player's panel pools every match they have in it. Win rate, serve placement, rally length, break points, returns and point endings are counted directly from those points; nothing is estimated. Matches still being tagged, doubles, and intra-squad practice sets are left out. Counts are shown next to every rate because samples range from one match to six.
